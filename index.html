FAQ

Common questions

Is this a real exchange?

No. Safu Traders is a front-end demonstration running entirely on mock data in your browser. No real funds are ever moved.

Why do deposits need approval?

It mirrors how custodial platforms reconcile on-chain transfers. Here the admin plays the role of the treasury desk.

How do I get started?

Create an account, then request a deposit from the wallet. Once the admin approves it, your balance is credited and you can trade, stake or predict.

Can I undo an admin action?

Yes. Every admin mutation pushes a snapshot, and the Undo button in the panel restores the previous state.
